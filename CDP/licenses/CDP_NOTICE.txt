CDP NOTICE - CDP category of Praat AudioTools
=============================================

CDP_Processes.tsv, CDP_Parameters.tsv) are part of Praat AudioTools and
are released under its MIT licence.

OPEN ITEM BEFORE PUBLIC RELEASE
-------------------------------
LGPL-2.1 section 6 requires the source that corresponds to the shipped
binaries. The CDP8 repository is the published source of Release 8, but
the exact commit/tag matching these March 2023 builds has not been
identified yet. Before public distribution, either
  (a) confirm with the CDP maintainers which CDP8 tag/commit these builds
      come from and record it here, or
  (b) ship binaries rebuilt from a recorded CDP8 commit instead.
Until then, this package is suitable for development and testing.
